Who we are

Rooted in Nigerian soil, built for wider markets

Agnovyra Ventures is a registered agriculture and agribusiness enterprise based in Odeda, Ogun State. On our 10-acre farm in Abeokuta, every part supports another: cocoa and plantain, poultry, a piggery, fish ponds and a black soldier fly unit.

Beyond the farm, we're building an import and export arm, so that quality Nigerian produce can reach buyers abroad and farmers here can get better inputs.

Agnovyra Ventures is a business of Odusade Synergies Limited.

How our farm works

A circle where nothing is wasted

  1. Farm waste becomes foodCrop and kitchen waste goes to our black soldier fly larvae.
  2. Larvae become proteinThe larvae are a rich, natural protein for our birds and fish.
  3. Animals feed the soilManure and BSF frass go back to the land as organic fertiliser.
  4. The soil feeds our cropsCocoa and plantain grow on that fertility, and their waste starts the loop again.
